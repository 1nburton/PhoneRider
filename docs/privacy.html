Privacy Policy

Sketch Rider

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Sketch Rider is a track-building rider game. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Summary

Sketch Rider does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data, and does not operate its own user database.

The app uses local device storage for gameplay and settings, Google AdMob for ads, and Apple App Store services for in-app purchases.

Information Stored On Your Device

Sketch Rider may save gameplay information on your device, such as custom tracks, demo progress, settings, selected rider options, and purchase-unlock state. This information is used to make the app work and to preserve your experience between sessions.

Locally saved data is not sent to a Sketch Rider server. You can remove locally stored data by deleting the app from your device.

Advertising

Sketch Rider uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google and its partners may collect or receive device information, identifiers, IP address, app activity, crash or diagnostic information, and ad interaction data to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads.

Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can also learn more about Google advertising here: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

In-App Purchases

Sketch Rider may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Sketch Rider receives purchase status information needed to unlock purchased content, but it does not receive your full payment card number.

Apple explains its App Store privacy practices here: App Store & Privacy.

Analytics And Crash Data

Sketch Rider does not currently operate a separate analytics service. Apple may provide developers with aggregated crash and performance information depending on your device settings.

Children

Sketch Rider is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the issue.

Your Choices

  • You can delete locally stored app data by deleting Sketch Rider from your device.
  • You can manage ad personalization choices in your device settings and through Google privacy controls.
  • You can manage purchases and subscriptions, if any, through your Apple ID settings.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions about Sketch Rider, please contact the developer through the GitHub repository: https://github.com/1nburton/PhoneRider/issues.
